# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate, critique ideas rather
than people, welcome different levels of experience, and protect private or client information.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed
to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
